Questions

Before you reserve.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?

Yes. The KAIRO app runs on iOS 17 and Android 12 or newer, and syncs with Apple Health and Health Connect.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Sleep, heart, recovery and trends are all included with the ring. There is nothing to renew.

Can I swim or shower with it?

Yes. The sealed titanium shell is rated to 100 m, so pools, the sea and the shower are all fine.

How do I find my size?

After you reserve, we post a free kit with eight plastic sizers. Wear one for a day, tell us the size, and your ring ships.

How does charging work?

Drop the ring onto the magnetic dock. It reaches full charge in about 70 minutes and lasts up to nine days.
